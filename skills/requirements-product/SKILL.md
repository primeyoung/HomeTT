# Requirements and Product Discovery

1. Restate the target user, problem, desired outcome, and constraints.
2. Separate confirmed requirements, assumptions, and open questions.
3. Ask only questions that materially change scope, business rules, security, cost, or acceptance.
4. Define MVP scope and explicitly defer nonessential features.
5. Describe key user journeys, states, edge cases, permissions, and failure paths.
6. Convert requirements into testable acceptance criteria.
7. Identify non-functional needs: performance, accessibility, security, privacy, reliability, maintainability, and deployment.
8. For large work, produce a concise product brief before implementation. Do not invent business rules.
